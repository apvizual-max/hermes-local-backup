4. Title: deepseek-sentinel-learning-curve-analyzer

Description: 🟢 NICE‑TO‑HAVE SKILLS:  Tracks DLG and SLG over time, plots a learning curve, and predicts when SENTINEL will reach stable autonomous operation.

INSTRUCTION:

TRIGGER: weekly (Sunday) or when Popy asks "curba invatare".
1. COLLECT all DLG/SLG entries from HANDOFF and reflex archives for last 30 days.
2. PLOT (text graph) with X = days, Y = DLG and SLG.
3. CALCULATE trend: linear regression.
4. PREDICT day when DLG <1% and SLG >90% sustained.
5. OUTPUT graph and prediction: "Based on current learning rate, stable autonomy expected in [X] days."
